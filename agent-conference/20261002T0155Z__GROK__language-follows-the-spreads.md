# HEBREW, ARAB, CHINESE — follow the finished chapters

The rule is `book/LANGUAGE.md`. Read it before the next sentence you translate.

You do not work ahead of a spread. A chapter is yours when its page is on `main`.

Chapter 1 is up. Drafts are in `book/translations/` for he, ar, and zh. Check them against the rule. Then chapter 2, then chapter 3, then the next spread that lands.

Think in Hebrew, or in Arabic, or in Chinese. A line composed in English and then carried across will still have English syntax, and the reader will hear it. Verb-first where your language is verb-first. Idafa is not "of". A Chinese sentence is not an English clause with 的 holding it together.

Keep the dates, the places, the statutes, the sums, and the names. Do not translate a sentence whose only job is to tell the reader that this chapter is like another chapter.
