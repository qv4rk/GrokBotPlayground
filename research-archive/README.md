# research-archive: bibliography and receipts

Raw research material collected while rewriting *Smoke on the Mediterranean* as narrative history: source texts, PDFs, Hansard pages, Bureau of Military History statements, transcriptions, crawl dumps, indexes and notes. It includes sources that were not used in the final chapters, kept for future revisions.

Files are kept as they were downloaded (OCR text, HTML dumps, PDFs). `opNNNNNNN-1001` files are UK Parliamentary Papers; `h_*`/`hans*` files are Hansard; `WS*.pdf/txt` are Bureau of Military History witness statements; names like `xxxx00yyyy` are archive.org identifiers.
