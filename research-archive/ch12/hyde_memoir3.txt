Presentation to Mr. S. O’Kelly, T.C.
A very interesting ceremony took place at
25 Parnell square last night, when Sean T.
O’Kelly, T.C., Sec. of the Gaelic League, was
presented by the members of the O’Rahilly Sinn
Fein Club, Blessington Street, with a beautifully
illuminated Celtic address (executed by Messrs.
Kelly, Burke, and Kane, the Frongoch artists),
on the occasion of his marriage. The address
was read by Mr. Gregg Murphy, and several
speakers testified to the lasting work which Mr.
O’Kelly had accomplished for Sinn Fein and the
Gaelic League. Mr. O’Kelly suitably acknow
ledged the presentation. A very successful
celidh followed.
Lights Not Screened.
At a Lincolnshire town, Lt. Edward Marshall
