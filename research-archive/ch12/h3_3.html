Cuimhní cinn a breacadh 1918-19 : an tríú cuid

ReadAboutContentsHelp
Douglas Hyde's memoir is in four parts, composed at various periods in 1918-19, mostly when he was ill and confined to bed. It looks back on various aspects of his career in the Irish language movement. Part 3 is 21 pages long and in it Hyde again discusses the politicisation of the Coiste Gnótha or Executive Committee, the nature of this committee, and the gradual weakening of his own position.

Presentation to Mr. S. O’Kelly, T.C.

A very interesting ceremony took place at 25 Parnell square last night, when Sean T. O’Kelly, T.C., Sec. of the Gaelic League, was presented by the members of the O’Rahilly Sinn Fein Club, Blessington Street, with a beautifully illuminated Celtic address (executed by Messrs. Kelly, Burke, and Kane, the Frongoch artists), on the occasion of his marriage. The address was read by Mr. Gregg Murphy, and several speakers testified to the lasting work which Mr. O’Kelly had accomplished for Sinn Fein and the Gaelic League. Mr. O’Kelly suitably acknowledged the presentation. A very successful celidh followed.

Lights Not Screened. At a Lincolnshire town, Lt. Edward Marshall

Independent May 5 1918
