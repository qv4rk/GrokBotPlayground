 WAR.
IRISH INSTITUTIONS (ECONOMIES).
 HC Deb 21 December 1915 vol 77 c201W
 201W
 §
 Mr. GINNELL
 asked the Secretary to the Treasury if he will specify the Irish institutions in the expenditure on which economies in the coming year have been so far determined, with the amount in each case; and whether further economies in Irish institutions are in contemplation?
 §
 Mr. MONTAGU
The Estimates for the coming financial year are now under consideration, and will be published in due course.
