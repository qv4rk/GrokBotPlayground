 MUNITIONS.
ARREST OF MR. SHEEHY SKEFFINGTON.
 HC Deb 21 June 1915 vol 72 c948W
 948W
 §
 Mr. LYNCH
 asked the Chief Secretary for Ireland whether Mr. Sheehy Skeffington is wearing prison clothes and doing hard labour; whether he is being forcibly fed; and what is his present physical condition?
 §
 Mr. BIRRELL
Sheehy Skeffington was discharged temporarily on the 15th instant, on account of health. The answers to the first two parts of the question are accordingly in the negative, and with regard to the last part I have no information.
