                THE     VOLUNTEER                    AID        ASSOCIATION.




                         There               were      many        citizens            of     Dublin       who were

       of        such    ultra-respectability                                and       loyalty          that         they

       could           not         be    recruited              into       the       Irish       Volunteers.

       These           were         brought            in     to     an    organisation                 called            the

       Volunteer                Aid          Association,                 which       was      organised

       principally                      by    Henry.        Harrison.                  They      took      an office

       at        44,    Dawson               Street,          and        collected            subscriptions

       for        the    Volunteers.                          This        was       their.    sole        purpose


                             This        organisation                    must     have        been      started

           in    the    middle               of    the      summer         of     1914,        and      after         a

       brief           career            it       disappeared               after       we broke           with

       Mr.        Redmond.


                             Mr.        Aliaga         Kelly,            wine     merchant,             was

       Treasurer,                   and       among         other         members            were    Sir        John

       Lynch,           of         O'Keeffe            & Lynch,            Solicitors,               Molesworth

       Street,               Sir        John        O'Connell,             Solicitor,               and     Mr.

       Brendan               Fottrell,               who was             afterwards            killed           in

           France.
