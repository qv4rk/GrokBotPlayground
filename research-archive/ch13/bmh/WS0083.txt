                                   The      Death         of        The      O'Rahilly,                  1916.




                            With           regard         to        the      death           of     The        O'Rahilly,
                                                                                                                                          I
        heard          that           Albert        Mitchell,                    who runs            a Comb and                   Bead

                            in                             Street,                Dublin,            Offered               his     services,
       Factory                      Waterford
        when          the        Insurrection                   broke            out,        as     an     ambulance               driver           to

        the       British              Forces.                  He was            accepted,                and        in    the      course

        of      his     work           he        came upon               O'Rahilly                lying          wounded           in     Moore

        street,              or       perhaps                      Lane.                    Mitchell              attempted                to
                                                                Moore

        pick          him        up and           take     him            into        the     ambulance                time        after

        time,          but         on each              occasion             was          prevented              by British

        military.                         Eventually,                when         O'Rahilly                was        dead,        Mitchell

        was       allowed              to        pick     up the             body,           and,        knowing            that

        O'Rahilly's                       personal             possessions                   would             be seized            by the

        first          soldier               who got            an        opportunity,                   Mitchell                took         the

        contents                 of    O'Rahilly's                   pockets,                including                his        watch,
                                                                                                                                                    and
        the       following                  day        gave        them         to       Madame         O'Rahilly.


                             This          is     the          story         as       I     heard         it     at    the        time,         and

        I       hope        that       Mitchell                is    still            alive         to         corroborate              and

        amplify              it.
