                          Statement                by     Bulmer           Hobson.




                 From          the    beginning                 there           were       two       honorary

Secretaries                - Eoin             MacNeill               and       Lawrence           Kettle,            the

former          of     whom acted                  also        as Chairman                 of     the      Provisional

Committee.


                 In       addition,                there        was a paid                 assistant.            Secretary

from      early           in     1914.


                 In       January             of    that        year           went
                                                                               I             to      the    U.S.A.           to

contact          Count          Von       Bernstorff                  with       a view           to      interesting

Germany          in       Ireland,             bringing               with       me a document                   drawn        up

by      Casement           somewhat                on the         lines          of    his        later         document

"Ireland,              Germany            and       the        next        War".


                 When I           left         Ireland            the          headquarters                of    the

Volunteers                were       at    my private                  office          at       12     D'Olier         Street,

Dublin.


                 On my return                      from        the                     the        headquarters
                                                                       U.S.A.
had      been        transferred                   to     an    office           in    206        Great         Brunswick

Street,          now Pearse                Street,              and        there       I     found         Liam       Gogan,

one      of    the        members          of       the        Provisional                 Committee,

installed             in       a secretarial                    capacity.                   Whether             he    was

paid      or     not       I    do not             know,        but        I    presume           that      he       was

as     MacNeill            and       Kettle             were     still           and       continued             to    be

joint         Honorary            Secretaries.


                Gogan,           a young                graduate,               was not           equal         to    the
