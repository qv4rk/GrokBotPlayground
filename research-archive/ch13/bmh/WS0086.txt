                     Statement                 by     Bulmer            Hobson




                        About           twenty-five                per        cent,      of       the     material
                                                                                                   adventure
   in   Robert          Monteith's                  book        "Casement's              Last'

   relates        to        matters            within           my own knowledge                     and       in

   every      case          I    have         found        that         he    has given             a simple

   straightforward                      accurate            account             of     what       actually

  happened.                     In    this,         his     book         is     an     exception           to       the

  majority         of           books         which        have         been     published              on the         same

  period.


                        I       may say         that        this         tribute         to       Monteith's

  book       is   not           based         on any        personal             interest            in    the

  author,         but           merely         on my recognition                        of    the       fact        that

  it    is    reliable                in      so far        as     it        relates         to    facts        within

  my personal                   knowledge.
