Same Estate. — 18 January 1854. 

Mr. Sadleir proposing to lodge the balance of his purchase money, including that of Mr. Stapleton 
who purchased in trust for him, as follows: 19,000 1. during this month, 10,0001. in each of the 
months of February, March, and April, and the residue in Slay ; let the unpaid purchase money 
be lodged at the times above specified, and no further proceedings take place on conditional order 
for attachment, unless, on application of the parties interested in the fund, it shall appear that the 
immediate lodgment of the whole, or any larger sums than those above mentioned, is necessary 



Same Estate. — 11 February 1854. 

Mr. Sadleir undertaking to lodge 2,000 1. more within a week, and the residue according to 
the Order of the 18th January 1854; let him be at liberty now to take the conveyance of 
Lot No. 1, of manor of Cahir; the deed to be retained in Court until after the lodgment of the 
2,000 1 . ; the Master to be at liberty to pass the conveyance in its course. 



Same Estate. — 9 March 1854. 

On application, on behalf of John Sadleir, for provisional credit, refuse the motion, with costs. 
On motion of petitioner’s solicitor, refuse the motion. Mr. Sadleir to lodge 2,200 1. by Monday 
next.
