INCUMBERED ESTATES (IRELAND) COMMISSION. 



Poor Law Commission Office, Dublin, 
Sir, 8 July 1850. 

I am directed by the Commissioners for administering the Laws for the Relief 
of the Poor in Ireland to acknowledge the receipt of your letter of the 2d instant, 
transmitting a Return furnished by the Incumbered Estates Commission, in 
pursuance of an Order of The House of Commons, and requesting the Com- 
missioners to state the amount of the Poor Law Valuation of the several 
Townlands included in such, Return; and, in reply, I am to state, that the 
Commissioners are unable to identify the Townlands, unless it be stated in what 
Union and Electoral Division they are situate. 

The Return is enclosed herewith. 

I have, &c. 

T. N. Redington, Esq., (signed) W. Stanley, 

&c. &c. &c. Sec^ 



' Incumbered Estates Office, 

Sir, 10 July 1850. 

In reference to your letter of the 9th instant, transmitting a copy of a letter 
from the Secretary to the Commissioners for administering the Laws for the Relief 
of the Poor in Ireland, requiring to be informed in what Union and Electoral 
Divisions are situated the Townlands included in the Return recently furnished 
by me in obedience to an Order of The House of Commons ; I am to state, that 
the Commissioners are unable to supply the information required— the rentals, 
as prepared by them, referring only to the Baronies in which the lands are 
situate, and not to the Unions or Electoral Divisions. 

I have, &c. 

T. N. Redington, Esq., (signed) S. Woulfe Flanagan , 

&c. &c. &c. S ec y. 



N.B' The column for T he Poor Law Valuation of each Lot by Townland ” has not been filled 
up, there being no means in the Office of the Commissioners for ascertaining same. The column 
for “ Ordnance Valuation of each Lot, &c.” has been filled up as far as practicable. The Ordnance 
Valuation (or Mr. Griffith's Valuation) not having been completed as yet in the Counties of Cork, 
Dublin, Kerry, Limerick, Tipperary and Waterford, wherever the Estates sold lie in those Counties, 
the Return is necessarily imperfect. 



29 June 1850. 



(signed) S. Woulfe Flanagan,
