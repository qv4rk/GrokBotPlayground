I HAVE the honour to acknowledge the receipt of your letter of the 
27th July, wherein you state that their Excellencies the Lords Justices requested

to be furnished to them such information as would show the nature of the 
proceedings taken by them under the Act constituting the Court, the progress 
that has been made in carrying that Act into effect, and such other matters as 
might tend to exliibit the character and extent of the business transacted before 
them. 

In compliance with the request so contained in yom- letter, I am directed by 
the Commissioners for the Sale of Encumbered Estates in Ireland, to submit to 
you, for the information of the Lords Justices, the following details relative to 
the progress made by the Commissioners in proceeding under the Act of the 
13 Viet. c. 77. 

On the 28th .of August 1849, the Commissioners having been previously 
sworn met for the first time, and in compliance with the provisions of the 
Statute, proceeded to frame rules for the regulation of the practice in their 
Court. » 

On the 18th of September in the same year, those rules were submitted to 
the Privy Council in Ireland for their approbation, and on the 17th of October 
these rules were approved of by the Privy Council. 

On the 25th of the same month ol' October the Court of Commissioners was 
first opened for the despatch of public business.
