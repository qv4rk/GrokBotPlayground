any Finance Committee or Relief Committee under this Act, or other 10 
person in whose hands the collection of any rates, grants, loans or 
voluntary contributions, or the providing for, ordering’, management, 
control, or direction of the relief of the destitute poor under the pro- 
visions of this Act, shall or may be placed, shall, either in his own 
name, or the name of any other person, provide, furnish or supply for 15

of Record at Dublin.
