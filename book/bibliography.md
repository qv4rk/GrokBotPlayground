\#\#\# GENERAL EDITORIAL NOTE ON SOURCES & CITATION METHOD  
This bibliography is organized chapter by chapter (Chapters 1–27), matching the structure of \*Smoke on the Mediterranean: Ireland, Palestine, and the Imperial Machinery of Divide and Conquer\*. For each chapter, sources are strictly bifurcated into:  
1\. \*\*Primary Archival & Statutory Sources\*\*: Government statutes, parliamentary blue books, official Hansard debates, diplomatic dispatches, imperial court registers (\*sijillat\*), and contemporary archival depositories—equipped with direct, canonical URLs to open-access digital repositories (legislation.gov.uk, UK Parliamentary Archives/Hansard, US State Department Foreign Relations \[FRUS\], The Avalon Project at Yale Law School, UNISPAL, National Archives of Ireland, and Internet Archive/Project Gutenberg digital facsimiles).  
2\. \*\*Secondary Monographs & Scholarly Literature\*\*: Academic peer-reviewed monographs, foundational economic histories, and journal articles documenting specific empirical and regional historiographies.
